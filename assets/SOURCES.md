## Original illustration

`art/paper-planes.png` was generated for this project with OpenAI's built-in imagegen tool on 2026-10-17. Prompt summary: transparent paper-collage illustration of one blush-pink and one periwinkle-blue folded paper airplane, linked by a heart-shaped dotted flight trail with cream clouds and small golden sparkles; no text, people, brand, or watermark.

`decor/finish-heart.svg` was drawn locally for the V3 center finish on 2026-10-18. It is an original pink-and-blue heart illustration, not a copied character or external asset.
